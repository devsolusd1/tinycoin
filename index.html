What is TINYCOIN

The most valuable token on Solana.

TINYCOIN was created with the absolute minimum supply technically possible on Solana — just 1 raw unit. With 9 decimals, that equals only 0.00000001 TINYCOIN in total existence.

No other token on Solana can claim a smaller supply. This makes TINYCOIN the scarcest SPL token ever deployed — and scarcity is the foundation of value.

The mint authority is permanently revoked. Supply is locked forever.

How it works

The math behind scarcity.

What is a Burn?

Burning sends tokens to a dead wallet — an address no one controls. Those tokens are permanently removed from circulation, reducing total supply forever.

📉

Every burn makes TINYCOIN more scarce. Starting from the world's smallest supply and going lower — a one-way street toward absolute rarity.
